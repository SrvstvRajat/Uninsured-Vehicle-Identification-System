Car stolen from Rohini Sector 9; police check CCTV

A silver Hyundai i20 bearing registration number DL07AG5107 was stolen from Rohini Sector 9 in Delhi during October 2024. The owner discovered the theft in the morning and informed the police. Investigators are examining CCTV footage from nearby shops. According to police data, 17 vehicles have been reported stolen in the city this month.
