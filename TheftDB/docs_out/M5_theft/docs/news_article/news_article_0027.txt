Vehicle thefts rise in Delhi

Delhi: Police have recorded 12 cases of vehicle theft in January 2026, a noticeable rise over the previous months. In one of the recent cases, a silver Honda Amaze with registration number DL01AA7685 was stolen from Indirapuram. Officers said most vehicles were taken from residential lanes at night. The police have increased night patrolling and appealed to residents to use steering locks and park in well-lit places.
