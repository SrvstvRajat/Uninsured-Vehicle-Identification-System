Vehicle thefts rise in Delhi

Delhi: Police have recorded 9 cases of vehicle theft in May 2026, a noticeable rise over the previous months. In one of the recent cases, a grey Tata Nexon with registration number DL06AF8878 was stolen from Lajpat Nagar. Officers said most vehicles were taken from residential lanes at night. The police have increased night patrolling and appealed to residents to use steering locks and park in well-lit places.
