Vehicle thefts rise in Bengaluru

Bengaluru: Police have recorded 15 cases of vehicle theft in July 2025, a noticeable rise over the previous months. In one of the recent cases, a black Maruti Swift with registration number TN65GW5304 was stolen from Sector 62. Officers said most vehicles were taken from residential lanes at night. The police have increased night patrolling and appealed to residents to use steering locks and park in well-lit places.
